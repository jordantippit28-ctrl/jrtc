Significant figures (14 points)
5. State two rules for counting significant figures. (2 pts)
6. Report each value to three significant figures. (6 pts — 2 pts each)
a) 0.004567 → ______
b) 45,672 → ______
c) 12.0004 → ______
7. Perform and report with correct significant figures. (6 pts — 2 pts each)
a) 4.56 × 1.4 = ______
b) (12.01 + 3.1 + 0.002) = ______
c) 0.00450 ÷ 2.0 = ______
Dimensional analysis (12 points)
8. Show by dimensional analysis whether v = at is dimensionally consistent. (3 pts)
9. Convert 90 km/h to m/s using unit factors. (3 pts)
10. For T = 2π sqrt(L/g), use dimensional analysis to confirm T has units of time. (3 pts)
11. Given A has units kg·m/s, B has units s-1, and C has units m2, find base units of X = A·B2/C. (3 pts)
Density problems (18 points)
12. Define density and give its SI unit. (2 pts)
13. Metal block: mass 2.50 kg, volume 3.20 × 10-3 m3. Calculate density in kg/m3 and g/cm3. (6 pts)
14. Cylindrical rod: length = 0.50 m, radius = 2.0 cm, mass = 5.00 kg. Calculate density (show volume). (5 pts)
15. Sample: mass = 85.0 g, displaced volume = 9.50 mL. Find density and say whether it could be aluminum (ρ ≈ 2.70 g/cm3). (5 pts)
Percent error (14 points)
16. Define percent error and write its formula. (2 pts)
17. Accepted g = 9.80665 m/s2. Measured g = 9.72 m/s2. Calculate percent error. (3 pts)
18. Student measures 50.00 g weight as 49.84 g. Calculate percent error and state what the sign means. (3 pts)
19. Trials: 9.78, 9.80, 9.83 m/s2. Compute mean and percent error vs 9.80665. (6 pts)
Bonus (optional, 6 pts)
20. A hydrometer sinks until displaced fluid mass equals its mass. Explain briefly (2–3 sentences) how changing the hydrometer’s shape (but not its mass) affects sensitivity of reading. (6 pts)
Scoring guide (suggested): SI base units 8, Metric prefixes 12, Sig figs 14, Dimensional analysis 12, Density 18, Percent error 14, Bonus 6 = 100.
